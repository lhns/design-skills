# Teaching lab sample tracking: design record

Written by the designer with Rosa, the teaching lab manager, in sessions on 2026-01-12, 2026-01-19 and 2026-01-26.

## Context
Undergraduates in the biology practicals freeze samples in a shared -20 °C freezer and come back for them in later sessions. Tubes are labelled by hand, labels get lost, and students spend a long time searching. A terminal with a barcode scanner sits beside the freezer.

## What Rosa said
- 2026-01-12: "Students spend ten minutes hunting for their tubes in the freezer."
- 2026-01-12: "For each sample I need the student, the practical and the date it was frozen."
- 2026-01-19: "Printed labels, please. Nobody can read the handwriting."
- 2026-01-19: "Please don't make students type those long sample IDs. They get them wrong every time."
- 2026-01-19: "The scanner at the freezer dies a couple of times a term, and then the whole class is stuck."
- 2026-01-26: "Samples older than one term get thrown out."

## Constraints
- C1: Each sample records the student, the practical and the freeze date. (Rosa, 01-12)
- C2: Students find their tubes quickly. (Rosa, 01-12)
- C3: No manual entry of sample IDs anywhere. (Rosa, 01-19)
- C4: The freezer scanner fails a few times a term. (Rosa, 01-19)
- C5: Samples older than one term are discarded. (Rosa, 01-26)
- C6: Tube labels are printed, not handwritten. (Rosa, 01-19)

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Ruled out by | Choice | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 Tube label | printed barcode label; printed plain number; handwritten number | handwritten number: C6 | printed barcode label, freezer-grade (designer default, so the scanner can read it) | open |
| D2 Freezer layout | one rack per practical; one rack per student group | - | one rack per practical, rack and slot printed on the label (designer default) | open |
| D3 When the scanner is down | type a short code at the terminal; wait until the scanner is fixed, keeping samples in the fridge meanwhile | short code: C3 | wait until the scanner is fixed | decided |
| D4 End of term | the terminal lists samples older than a term for staff to discard; samples kept until Rosa decides | samples kept: C5 | the terminal lists them for staff to discard | decided |

## Design
When a student freezes a sample, the terminal prints a barcode label showing the student, practical, date, and the rack and slot to use (D1, D2). Students find a sample by its rack and slot (D2) and scan its label when they take it out (D1). If the scanner is down, students leave new samples in the fridge until it is fixed (D3). At the end of each term the terminal lists every sample older than a term, and staff remove them (D4).
