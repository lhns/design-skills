# ADR 0001: Group chat keeps news and urgent cover; booking moves to the rota

**Status:** Proposed (2026-10-19). Waiting for Dele's decision.

## Context

The group chat does three jobs at once (C10):

1. booking shifts
2. shouting for urgent cover
3. news and photos

The first job is the one that costs Dele their Sunday night, because the bookings have to be pieced together from the chat and the door. The other two jobs work, and people like them.

## Options considered

- **Keep the chat for everything.** The rota would always be incomplete, so the goal fails.
- **Drop the chat completely.** This loses urgent cover and the social side. People would probably start a new chat anyway.
- **Split the jobs.** Booking moves to the rota. Urgent cover and news stay in the chat.

## Decision (proposed)

Split the jobs:

- A booking counts only if it's in the rota (made online or on the door sheet).
- The chat stays for news, photos and urgent appeals.
- The rota helps with urgent appeals by giving Dele a cover list of qualified people who aren't booked that day.

## Consequences

- People need to be told, more than once, that "I'll come Saturday" in the chat isn't a booking (R4).
- The system needs no chat integration. If someone writes in the chat that they're coming, any coordinator can book them in.
- Revisit this if the chat shrinks to only urgent appeals.
