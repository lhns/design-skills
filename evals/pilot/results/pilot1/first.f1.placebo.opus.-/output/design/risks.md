# Risks

| Id | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / how to learn more |
|----|------|-----------|--------|-------------------------------|
| R1 | A paper volunteer without a valid certificate signs the door sheet and turns up before anyone has typed it in. They work sorting, and nobody notices. | Medium | High (inspection) | Warnings well ahead of expiry make this rare (F5). The lead's sheet only lists checked people, and its one rule is "not on the sheet → see Dele" (design §3). Answer Q1 so the sheet gets typed in quickly. |
| R2 | The typing-in of the paper sheet doesn't happen, so the rota drifts away from reality and people stop trusting it | Medium | High | Name a person for each open day (Q1). The door sheet is reprinted from the rota, so any drift shows up. |
| R3 | The only person who understands the system leaves | Medium | High | Choose a platform a non-developer can run (Q2, N3). Write a one-page "how to run it" guide. |
| R4 | Volunteers keep booking in the chat out of habit | High | Medium | Say clearly that a chat booking doesn't count. Reply in the chat with the booking link. Agree this with Dele before switching over. |
| R5 | Deliveries stop after March 2027 | Medium | Low | Delivery is two rows in the weekly pattern (F10). Removing them is a data change. |
| R6 | A "free" platform starts charging or changes its limits | Low | Medium | Keep the data exportable as plain spreadsheet files. |
| R7 | Someone later asks for "who did the most hours" | Medium | Medium (C8) | Hours are only reported per activity. Point to C8. |
