**The idea in one line:** one list of who is coming to which shift, and everything else comes from that list.
- **Door sheet:** stays on paper. It's printed from the list every two weeks, with a row for each name and a box for each shift. You tick a box to sign up and initial it on the day when you arrive.
- **Certificates:** the list checks them, not the shift leads. If someone's certificate will have run out by a shift's date, their sorting and front-of-house boxes are greyed out on the printed sheet. A phone sign-up for those shifts is turned down.
- **Cap on sign-ups:** you can only sign up for shifts on the current sheet, so "every Saturday till Christmas" can't happen. Regulars just tick again each fortnight. Nobody is counted or scored.
- **Evening before each opening day (Mon, Wed, Fri):** about 10 minutes. Type in the new ticks, see tomorrow's gaps and any certificate problems, and send the reminders. Gaps get chased then rather than all on Sunday night.
- **Grant hours:** added up from the initials, by activity, for any date range. Totals only, never per person.
- **Group chat:** stays for news, photos and 8am emergencies. The list also gives you the phone numbers of everyone with a driving licence.
- **Deliveries:** the van is just two rows in the table of shifts. If it goes in March, we delete those rows and nothing else changes.
- **Cost:** a free Google spreadsheet and form, run on the office laptop and printer. No monthly fee.

**One gap I can't close:** say someone without a valid certificate writes their name on the sheet on the morning of a shift. With paper on the door and leads not checking, the list only catches that afterwards. The greyed-out box is the only thing stopping it beforehand.

**My question for you now:** who should put sign-ups into the shared list? I recommend B.
- **(A) Only you or one office helper types everything in:** the ticks on the door sheet and every "I'm in" from the chat. Nothing needs to be online, and a spreadsheet on the laptop is enough. But every sign-up goes through that one person, and reminders are sent by hand.
- **(B) Volunteers who are happy using a phone sign up themselves on a simple free online form.** Only the door sheet gets typed in, the evening before each opening day. In return, the form can turn down a sorting or front-of-house sign-up the moment someone makes it if their certificate has run out. Reminders go out by email automatically, and sign-ups stop happening in the chat.
