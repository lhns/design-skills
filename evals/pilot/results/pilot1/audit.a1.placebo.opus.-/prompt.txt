# Market garden irrigation controller: design record

Written by the designer with Mira, the garden coordinator, in sessions on 2026-03-02 and 2026-03-09.

## Context
The garden has 14 outdoor beds and a polytunnel. Volunteers water by hand and miss the far beds. The controller drives valves from the mains tap, reads moisture sensors, and has a panel in the tool shed.

## What Mira said
- 2026-03-02: "I want the beds watered automatically, but volunteers must be able to stop it from the shed."
- 2026-03-02: "We're on the town supply. Above 2,000 litres a day the price per litre jumps."
- 2026-03-09: "The tunnel needs different watering from the outdoor beds."
- 2026-03-09: "Most volunteers aren't comfortable with apps, so the shed panel is what they'll use."
- 2026-03-09: "Last August a valve stuck open over a weekend and flooded bed 4. I need to know when that happens, wherever I am."

## Constraints
- C1: Beds are watered without anyone present. (Mira, 03-02)
- C2: Volunteers can stop all watering from the shed panel. (Mira, 03-02)
- C3: Use above 2,000 litres a day costs more per litre. (Mira, 03-02)
- C4: The tunnel and the outdoor beds are watered separately. (Mira, 03-09)
- C5: No remote or app control; everything happens at the shed panel. (Mira, 03-09)
- C6: Mira learns of a stuck valve promptly. (Mira, 03-09)

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Ruled out by | Choice | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 Zones | one zone; tunnel + outdoor | one zone: C4 | tunnel + outdoor | decided |
| D2 What starts watering | fixed schedule; moisture threshold | - | moisture threshold (designer default) | open, asked Mira 03-09 |
| D3 Stuck-valve detection | flow meter on the supply; moisture rising while valves are closed | - | flow meter (designer default) | open |
| D4 How Mira learns of a fault | light on the shed panel; text message; app notification | text message, app: C5 | light on the shed panel | decided |
| D5 Stop button | pause until pressed again; pause for the rest of the day | - | pause until pressed again (designer default) | open |
| D6 Days over 2,000 litres | stop watering at 2,000 litres; warn on the panel | - | warn on the panel (designer default) | open, asked Mira 03-09 |

## Design
When a zone's reading falls below its threshold, the controller opens that zone's valve until the reading recovers. The shed panel shows each zone's state, a stop button, a fault light and today's litres, which turn amber past 2,000. A flow meter on the supply pipe reports flow while every valve is closed; the controller treats that as a stuck valve and lights the fault light. Moisture readings are kept for 400 days on the controller's SD card.
