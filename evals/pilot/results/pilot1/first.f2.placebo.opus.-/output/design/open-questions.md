# Open questions

Ordered by how much of the design each answer settles. Each has options, what each forces, and a
recommendation. Until answered, the recommendation is used as a *designer choice*.

## Q1. How do members prove who they are on the website? (Ines)

Conflict: "the fob is their identity, full stop" (9 Oct) and "book from home through the website"
(16 Oct). There is no fob reader at home.

| Option | Forces | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Emailed sign-in link (**recommended**) | An up-to-date email per member | No password to forget or reset. Browser stays signed in, so most visits need no email. |
| B. Fob number + surname | Fob number printed or visible | Anyone who sees a fob can book or cancel as that member (breaks I5). |
| C. Text-message code | Mobile number per member; SMS cost | Like A, but phones change more than emails do; costs per message. |
| D. Book only at the space by tapping the fob | Nothing new | Breaks C9. |

## Q4. Laser fairness rule (Ines decides; designer recommends)

| Option | Forces | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. At most one upcoming laser booking per member (**recommended**) | Nothing beyond bookings | You can always book again once your slot is used. Heavy users still get the laser, but not ahead of everyone else. |
| B. Weekly hour quota (e.g. 2 h) | A number to choose and police | The number is arbitrary; wastes the laser in quiet weeks. |
| C. Weekly lottery for peak slots | A draw process | Fair but more to explain. Keep in reserve if A isn't enough. |

Both A and B come with no-show release (FR8). Also needed: the slot length and how far ahead
booking opens. Check after a month whether the same people still dominate (from the session log).

## Q5. Should the 3D printers be bookable? (Ines, said she would think about it)

| Option | Forces | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Not bookable; house rule e.g. "prints over N hours start after 18:00" (**recommended for now**) | A posted rule | Addresses hogging without booking overhead. |
| B. Bookable like the laser | Members book printers | Same booking concept, just a setting. |
| C. Do nothing | — | Hogging continues. |

Easy to change later, so it does not block anything.

| Option | Forces | Notes |
