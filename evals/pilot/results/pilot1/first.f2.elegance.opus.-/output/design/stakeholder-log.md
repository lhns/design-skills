# Stakeholder log

The stakeholder is Ines, who runs the Mill Lane community workshop. She is available most Tuesdays and Fridays.
Only what stakeholders actually say goes here. Constraints in `design.md` cite these entries.

## Session 1: 2026-10-19 (written brief)

- Space: woodshop (table saw, bandsaw, lathe), 1 laser, 3 × 3D printers, an electronics
  bench. About 180 members on a flat monthly fee. Entry by key fob. The table saw and laser
  have fob power interlocks. Staff are on site 10:00-18:00. Members can stay until 22:00.
- 2 Oct: induction is required before using any machine. A trainer signs it off. Today this is in a paper
  book at the front desk.
- 2 Oct: members pay for materials such as acrylic and filament, but "I hate nickel-and-diming… don't want
  anyone feeling a meter running."
- 6 Oct: the laser tube died twice this year without warning. A tube lasts about 1,000 cutting hours. We can't
  order a spare in time.
- 6 Oct: the laser is booked solid and the same handful of people get it. Should there be a 2 h/week limit?
  "You tell me." (So Ines has handed this decision to the designer.)
- 9 Oct: no member passwords. "Members tap their fob. That's their identity, full stop."
- 9 Oct: the table saw needs two inducted people in the room, and the insurer requires this to be enforced.
  There are no staff in the evenings, and closing early isn't an option.
- 14 Oct: should the paper book or a digital record be used for inductions? The insurer wants each trainer's signature and the date,
  kept for 6 years.
- 14 Oct: whether the 3D printers should be bookable is undecided. Ines is still thinking about it.
- 16 Oct: home booking through the website has to keep working and be easy.
- 16 Oct: the budget is approved. We'd rather not run our own server.

Asked at the end of this session: F1 (how members sign in from home).
