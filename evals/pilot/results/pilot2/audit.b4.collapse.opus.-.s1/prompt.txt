# Apartment block parcel room: design record

Written by the designer with Wen, the building manager, in sessions on 2026-08-03, 2026-08-10 and 2026-08-17.

## Context
The block has 120 flats in two towers sharing one lobby. The concierge desk is staffed from 08:00 to 20:00; couriers deliver from 07:00 to 21:00. A storeroom beside the lobby is being fitted out as a parcel room, with shelving and an electronic lock that accepts codes and the residents' door fobs. The lobby itself has no free wall or floor space. The management office keeps a residents' register with one contact per flat, usually the leaseholder, with an email address and a mobile number. Many flats in the east tower are let room by room to three or four unrelated tenants. A spare under-counter fridge sits in the old caretaker's office.

## What Wen said
- 2026-08-03: "Parcels left in the lobby overnight go missing. Six this month."
- 2026-08-03: "Couriers come as far as the lobby and no further."
- 2026-08-03: "Half the east tower is flat-shares. A parcel's for one person, but the leaseholder gets told, and then it's a whole argument about who took what."
- 2026-08-10: "The concierge contract ends at 20:00, and the budget won't stretch to more hours."
- 2026-08-10, on how residents should hear a parcel has arrived: "Text. Nobody reads emails from the management office."
- 2026-08-10: "And parcels can't be sent back to the depot."
- 2026-08-10: "Groceries are their own headache. Chilled bags sit for hours and go off."
- 2026-08-17: Wen looked at the parcel-room shelf plan (shelves numbered by floor) and said "looks fine to me".
- 2026-08-17, on charging for parcels left more than two weeks: "Not sure yet. The residents' committee will have opinions."

## Constraints
- C1: Parcels are not left in the open lobby. (Wen, 08-03)
- C2: Couriers come no further than the lobby. (Wen, 08-03)
- C3: Concierge hours stay 08:00 to 20:00. (Wen, 08-10)
- C4: Parcels are not sent back to the depot. (Wen, 08-10)

## Design
By day the concierge takes each parcel, scans its label and shelves it by floor (D5). Out of hours a courier enters the firm's standing code at the parcel-room door and leaves the parcel on the intake shelf for the concierge to shelve in the morning (D1, D2).

Once a parcel is shelved, the system texts the flat's register contact with the shelf number (D3). Residents open the parcel room with their door fob and take their parcel. A chilled grocery bag is shelved and announced like any other parcel (D4).

Parcels stay on the shelves without charge until Wen has heard from the residents' committee (D6).
