## My question for you
**Should a one-off booking only count once it's paid in full online at the time of booking?** Regulars would carry on as they do now.

I recommend yes. It answers your own question, and it's what makes January to March work: bookings pay for themselves and confirm themselves while you're away. It also settles how refunds, the booking service and cover while you're away should work.

Example: a family books the main hall for Saturday 13 March, 19:00–23:30. At £30 an hour that's £135.

- **(c) Paid at booking (recommended):** they pay £135 by card on 1 November, and the date shows as taken straight away. If they don't pay, nothing is booked and the date stays free for the wedding party. Many village halls using booking services work this way.
- **(b) Short hold:** the date is held for a few days and reopens by itself if they haven't paid.
- **(d) Booking fee:** say £30 when they book and the remaining £105 a few weeks before the date.
- **(a) As now:** pencilled in, with a cheque to come.

If you choose (c), the next decision is how many weeks' notice still gets a refund. I've suggested 8 weeks, but that number is yours to set.
