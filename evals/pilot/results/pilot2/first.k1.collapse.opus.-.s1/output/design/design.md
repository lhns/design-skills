# Village hall bookings: current design

Round 1, 2026-10-19. Owner: Gwen (bookings). Records: `vision.md` (her words), `kernel.yaml` (K constraints, X exclusions, W values), `forks.yaml` (F decisions), `concepts.yaml`.
Anything marked **forced** follows from what Gwen or the licensing officer said. Anything marked **suggested** is my recommendation on an open fork, and it stays open until Gwen (or the committee) decides.

## The idea in one line
**The only thing that takes a date is a booking in one public calendar, and the only thing that stops the music at night is a machine.** Everything else (payment, phone, kitchen) hangs off those two.

## 1. The 23:00 music limit: do this first (forced)
The licence allows amplified music "until 23:00 and not a minute after" (K3). The officer says hirer-dependent controls are not enough and the committee must be able to *show* enforcement (K5). A further breach risks a licence review (K6). Nobody goes to the hall or is on call at night (X2), and there's no money to pay a steward (K2). That rules out every option except a machine (F1):

- **The limiter's time switch cuts power to the sound-system sockets every night** (F1/f). It needs nobody present, and it doesn't depend on the hirer. The switch is already fitted (K4); it just needs setting.
- **The cut comes before 23:00**, early enough to cover the switch's drift between checks: suggested 22:55 (F3, K17).
- **The switch is in a locked cupboard** whose key never goes to hirers (F6). Hirers' keys from the shop don't change (X1).
- **No exceptions** for late events: the switch cuts every night (F7).
- **A record for the officer** (F5): one page saying what is cut, when, and who holds the cupboard key, plus a dated log of daytime checks (e.g. Gwen's first Monday of the month). Send the officer the page and ask them to confirm it meets the condition.

Open details: which sockets it must cover (F2, a question for the electrician; any wall socket in the hall or upstairs room that could run a sound system has to be on the switched circuit too), and whether to fit a switch that changes for summer time by itself (F4). The clocks go back on **2026-10-25**, and again on 2027-03-28 while Gwen is away.

**A real limit, not a planned step:** no switch can stop a battery-powered speaker. The switch is the standard control, but only the officer can say whether it's enough, which is another reason to send them the page.

## 2. One calendar of bookings (forced core)
- A **booking** is one room for one span of time for one hirer (K1). It's the only thing that takes a date, so a date can't be taken twice (W4).
- The **calendar** holds every booking, and anyone can see the free dates without asking Gwen (K7).
- The **rooms are a list** (K1): main hall with kitchen, and the small room. If the playgroup gets the small room full-time (F17), it simply becomes their standing booking and drops off the public calendar.
- A **standing booking** covers each regular (K8): lunch club Tuesdays, whist Thursday evenings, Pilates twice a week, dance school Saturday mornings. Gwen enters these once a year. Suggested: the regulars' extra dates (Christmas lunch, AGM, summer outing tea) go in the same annual call (F12), so they stop ringing for each one (K9).
- **Prices** are a table (K13): small room £12/h, main hall £25/h, £30/h on Saturday nights. The time the £30 rate starts is open (F18).

Suggested, open:
- **Paid at booking** (F9/c, *the question this round*): a one-off booking exists only once it's paid in full online. There's no pencilled state, so there's nothing to go quiet, no cheque to chase, and no date lost to a booking that comes to nothing (W3). This is the hire fee paid in advance, **not a deposit**: nothing is ever held back for damage (K14, W2).
- **Refunds** (F10): full refund if cancelled at least 8 weeks before, none after. Eight weeks is my guess at the time needed to re-let a Saturday, so it's Gwen's number to set.
- **Regulars** keep paying as now, billed per term by the treasurer (F11, K16).
- **A bought hall-booking service** (F15/c) holds the calendar, takes card payments, handles standing bookings and sends confirmations, so we don't build anything. It needs a small monthly fee.
- **Saturdays** (F16): recommended, Saturday afternoons and evenings for one-off hires and regulars on mornings only. Until the committee decides, first come, first served. Read literally, Gwen's twelve-a-year idea would take forty Saturdays from the dance school, so option b applies it to afternoons and evenings only.

## 3. No more calls at home
Email-only would shut out the lunch club and whist drive (K9), and calls at home must stop (K10). Suggested (F13/c): **one hall phone number** (a cheap mobile kept for the hall) with voicemail, answered on Monday and Wednesday mornings (K11). It's the only number published. Everyone who can books online.

**January to March** (F14, K12): by default nothing depends on a person. Online bookings pay and confirm themselves, the regulars' dates are already in, voicemail waits, and the treasurer handles refunds. If a committee member can cover the phone on set mornings, better.

## 4. Kitchen
Suggested (F19/a): **all food rubbish goes home with the hirer**, there's a short checklist on the kitchen door, and a hirer who leaves a mess can't book online again until the committee agrees. No money is held back (W2). Suggested (F20/a): a daytime look at the kitchen on Monday morning, before the Tuesday lunch club.

## 5. Keys: unchanged (X1)
Collection from the shop and return through its letterbox stay as they are. Open (F21): how the shop learns who is collecting, which matters while Gwen is away.

- Most one-off hirers can pay online (F9).
- The main hall has wall sockets outside the switched stage circuit (F2).
