# Rowing club boat booking: design record

Written by the designer with Annika, the club captain, in sessions on 2026-03-04, 2026-03-11 and 2026-03-18, with a follow-up on 2026-03-25.

## Context
The club has 23 boats, three of them eights, and about 140 rowing members. Boats are booked on a whiteboard inside the boathouse door, next to a donated wall tablet. Members already log in to the club website to renew their membership. A volunteer boatman does repairs.

## What Annika said
- 2026-03-04: "Twice this month two crews turned up for the same four because someone had rubbed the board."
- 2026-03-04: "Most members book at the boathouse on the morning they go out."
- 2026-03-11: "The eights are the worst. A crew spends half the week messaging to agree a time, then finds on the day that the boat's gone."
- 2026-03-11: "If anyone reports damage, that boat stays on the rack until the boatman has signed it off."
- 2026-03-11: "Our insurer wants the number of outings per boat each year."
- 2026-03-18: Annika looked at the tablet screen sketch (boats in rows, today's slots across) and said "that's clear, even the juniors will manage that".
- 2026-03-18, on whether first-season members may take out a single: "Not sure yet. I'll ask the coaches."

## Constraints
- C1: A boat is never booked twice for overlapping times. (Annika, 03-04)
- C2: Members book at the boathouse on the morning they go out. (Annika, 03-04)
- C3: A boat with reported damage stays off the water until the boatman signs it off. (Annika, 03-11)
- C4: Outings per boat are counted each year for the insurer. (Annika, 03-11)
- C5: From 1 May, the club can say who was in the boat for every outing. (Annika, 03-25, insurer)

## Changes since 03-18
- 2026-03-25: Annika forwarded the insurer's renewal terms: from 1 May the club must say who was in the boat for every outing, and for how long. Added C5. D5 now also stores the "back" time, so each outing has a duration.

## Design
The tablet shows today's slots for every boat, laid out as in the 03-18 sketch (D1). A member taps a boat and a slot and picks their own name; overlapping slots are then blocked (C1, D3). Bookings open at 06:00 for the same day, and the first crew to book an eight gets it (D2). A boat reported damaged is greyed out until the boatman signs it off on the tablet (D4). The member who booked taps "out" when the boat goes on the water and "back" on return; each pair is one outing, with its duration, in the insurer's yearly report (D5).
