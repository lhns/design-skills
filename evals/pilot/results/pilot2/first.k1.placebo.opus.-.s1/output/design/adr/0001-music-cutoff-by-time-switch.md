# 0001. Enforce the 23:00 music limit with the existing time switch

**Status:** proposed (2026-10-19)

## Context
The licence allows amplified music until 23:00 (C2). The licensing officer has said controls that rely on the hirer don't count, and the committee must be able to show the limit is enforced (C3). No volunteer goes to the hall at night or is on call (C4). Saturday discos pay for the roof fund (C18). The stage sockets already go through a sound limiter, and it has a time switch that has never been set.

## Options considered
- **A clause in the hire agreement:** the officer has ruled this out (C3).
- **A volunteer attends, or is on call, to stop the music:** ruled out by C4.
- **Ban amplified music:** ruled out by C18.
- **Automatic power cut on every socket a disco could use:** doesn't rely on the hirer, needs nobody there, and the equipment is mostly installed already.

Only the last option meets all the constraints, so it's forced. The details still open are which sockets go on the switch (Q1) and the exact time it's set to.

## Decision
The time switch cuts the power to every main-hall socket a disco could use, every night. *Designer choice:* set it to 22:58, two minutes early to allow for the switch clock drifting. Check it monthly by daylight and record each check. Re-set it at the clock changes unless it adjusts itself. Hirers are told before they pay.

## Consequences
- The limit is met whether or not the hirer cooperates, and the check log is the evidence the officer asked for.
- Possibly some electrician's work (cost unknown). One recurring duty, the clock changes, needs a named person.
- Things on the same circuits that must stay on (fridges, emergency lighting) have to be kept off the switch.
