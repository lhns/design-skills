# 0003. Saturday afternoons and evenings are kept for one-off bookings

**Status:** proposed (2026-10-19). Needs the committee.

## Context
Families can't get a Saturday for a christening tea or a child's party until spring (C15). Saturday-night parties pay for the roof fund (C18). The dance school has every Saturday morning. Pilates wants Saturday afternoons as well. Gwen wondered about a cap of 12 Saturdays per hirer per year.

## Options considered
- **12-Saturday cap per hirer:** would end the dance school's mornings, which don't get in the way of families or parties. It needs counting per hirer. And 12 isn't forced by anything.
- **No rule:** C15 stays unmet.
- **Regular bookings can't include Saturday after 12:00:** frees exactly the slots families and parties want. The dance school is untouched. Nothing to count, and it's checked once, when a series is set up.

## Decision (proposed)
No regular booking covers Saturday after 12:00. Pilates is offered another weekday slot.

## Consequences
- Saturday afternoons and evenings are open to families and parties.
- Pilates may be unhappy (R6).
- Where the line falls (12:00) is a designer choice. The dance school's actual finishing time should set it.
