Hello, Gwen here. I do the bookings for the village hall, and the committee has finally agreed I can have some help. Our treasurer passed on your name. I've pulled together the notes I made over the summer and a couple of emails, so forgive the jumble.

**The hall.** Main hall (seats 120, with a stage), a kitchen, and the small meeting room upstairs. We're all volunteers; the hall hasn't employed anyone since the caretaker retired, and there's no money to pay anybody now. The premises licence lets us have amplified music until 23:00 and not a minute after. When the stage sockets were put through a sound limiter a few years back, the electrician fitted a time switch on it too. As far as I know nobody has ever set it. Saturday-night parties pay for the roof fund, and round here a party without a disco isn't a party.

Everything lives in my diary at home. People ring me, I pencil them in, and every so often I find I've put two parties on one Saturday. What I'd really like is for people to see which dates are free without asking me.

Regulars: the lunch club on Tuesdays, the whist drive on Thursday evenings, Pilates twice a week, and the dance school, which has had every Saturday morning since 2022. The Pilates lady now wants Saturday afternoons as well. Meanwhile I'm telling families there's no Saturday for a christening tea or a child's party until next spring. Maybe nobody should get more than, oh, twelve Saturdays a year? Just thinking aloud.

July. Keys are fine as they are: hirers pick them up from the shop and put them back through the shop's letterbox. Please don't fix what isn't broken. None of us goes near the hall at night any more, and none of us is on call at night either, at the hall or from home. We agreed that when I took the diary on.

We used to take a hundred-pound deposit. In 2023 we held back the toddler group's deposit over a sticky floor and two broken glasses, and it ended up in the parish magazine: letters for three issues, and two committee members resigned. We scrapped deposits that spring, and it's been peaceful since.

August. The neighbours complained about a fortieth that went on past midnight. Here's what the licensing officer sent afterwards:

> From: Licensing team
